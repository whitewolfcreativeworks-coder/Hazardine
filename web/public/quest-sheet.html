Hazardine Quest Sheet

Optional quest mode, provisional rules. Two dungeons run at once: each side has its own three rooms on the battlefield, and the paths cross in the middle.

Rooms. Your next room is either of the two squares listed for it. Clear rooms in order.

Trial. When one of your pieces (not the king) ends a move on your next room, draw a card. Piece strength + card value must reach the target.

Card values. 2-4 = 1, 5-7 = 2, 8-10 = 3, J-Q = 4, K-A = 5. Strength: pawn 2, knight and bishop 4, rook 6, queen 9.

Pass: tick the room and take its boon. Fail: the piece goes to jail and the room stays open.

Boons. Ford: a rook, bishop or knight to deploy at home. Crypt: a shield (cross one out to turn a lost defence into a repelled attack). Vault: the quest is complete.

Quest wins variant: completing the Vault wins the game. Boons only variant: the Vault gives a queen and frees one prisoner instead. Linked variant: each time your rival clears a room, your next target drops by 1 (note it under Ease).
